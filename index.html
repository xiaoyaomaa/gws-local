gws CLI

Last updated: 4 November 2026

gws CLI is a personal command-line tool used by its owner to read and write their own Google Workspace data — Google Drive, Sheets, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Slides and Tasks.

Privacy policy

  • The tool is used only by its owner, on the owner's own machine.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally in an encrypted file on that machine.
  • No user data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any third-party server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Purpose

Personal productivity only: managing the owner's own spreadsheets, documents, calendar and mail. The tool is not offered as a service to third parties.
